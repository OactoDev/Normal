## Side Quest 1: Model Card

You teach your coding agent the same things every day. Let's see what that adds up to.

Upload your Claude Code or Codex session logs and Mubit turns them into a **model card**: the model your data would train, **plus** the patterns you keep repeating that should become a model or a skill.

### How to enter

1. **Find your session logs (JSONL files):**
   - **Claude Code:** `~/.claude/projects/`
   - **Codex:** `~/.codex/sessions/`
2. **In Discord, run** `/upload your@email.com` **with your own email**, then upload your files.
3. **Mubit emails you your model card.**
4. **Post your model card on X and LinkedIn.** Tag **@MubitLabs** on X and **Mubit** on LinkedIn. _(greatly appreciated)_
5. **Drop both post links in `#modelcards`.**

### How we pick the winners

Max reviews every model card and picks the winners against our internal judging criteria.

Open to registered Night Shift hackers. **One entry per person.**

### Prizes

1. Nintendo Switch 2
2. $100 in Mubit inference credits
3. $25 in Mubit inference credits

### Bonus

Route Claude Code or Codex through Mubit. Change your base URL to Mubit's and use your Mubit key, and every request shows up in your Mubit console.

### Privacy (edit at 08:53)

API keys and secrets are **redacted** before anything is processed. We never store them.
